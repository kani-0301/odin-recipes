Biriyani

The One Pot Wonder

Biriyani is a celebration of all that is great about Indian food – the heady aromas, the vibrant colours, the fluffy rice and those addictive curry flavours. Make this Chicken Biryani with your protein of choice – or try a vegetable biryani!

You’ll find variations of Biryani all across the Indian subcontinent, from Pakistan to Bangladesh, Afghanistan to India. There are 2 main types – one where the protein and/or vegetables are cooked mixed throughout the rice, and the other version known as Hyderabad-style biryani in India where meat and rice are layered and cook in a sealed pot over fire.

Ingredients

  • Spices
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Green chillis
  • Coriander
  • Mint leaves
  • Curd
  • Protein of choice
  • Basmati or Seeraga Sambha Rice
  • Masalas
  • Ginger Garlic paste

Steps for cooking

  1. Finely chop the onions and tomatoes
  2. Take a pot and heat it up with oil in it
  3. Add your onions and saute until golden brown follow up with gg paste
  4. Add your tomatoes and saute until they get cooked
  5. Add your spices and saute
  6. Add your masalas and water
  7. Add your protein and curd and let it simmer after mixing everything in
  8. Add rice and water in the ratio of 1:2
  9. Close it up and let it boil and cook until its cooked in 20-25 mins
